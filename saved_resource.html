About Steganography

What is Steganography?

Steganography is the practice of concealing a file, message, image, or video within another file, message, image, or video. Unlike cryptography, which focuses on making the content unreadable, steganography focuses on hiding the very existence of the data.

Our tool uses advanced steganographic techniques to securely hide your sensitive information within digital media files, making it virtually undetectable to unauthorized parties.

Types of Steganography

Image Steganography

Hiding data within image files by subtly altering pixel values, typically using the Least Significant Bit (LSB) technique.

Audio Steganography

Embedding secret messages in audio files by modifying imperceptible aspects of the sound wave.

Video Steganography

Concealing data within video files by exploiting the temporal dimension and frame-to-frame redundancy.
